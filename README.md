# NDLab01 - Ping va Traceroute bang Packet Tracer

Thu muc nay chua loi giai va cau hinh router cho bai tap NDLab01. Topology va dia chi mang duoc lay theo `NDLab01 - Packet Tracer Introduction (Requirements).docx.pdf`; quy trinh thuc hien theo file Instruction kem theo. Khong su dung vi du topology bon router khong lien quan trong Instruction.

## Lua chon cho bai tap

- Pod: `1` (Requirement cho phep chon gia tri nay neu chua duoc giao Pod).
- Dinh tuyen: static route theo ca hai chieu.
- Cong router: `Serial0/0/0` va `FastEthernet0/0`; chon model/module Packet Tracer co dung cac cong nay.
- Dau DCE: `R1-ISP Serial0/0/0`.

## Tep trong thu muc

- `NDLab01_Report.md`: topology, bang dia chi, giai thich CLI, bang kiem thu du kien, phan tich su co va cau tra loi van dap.
- `R1-ISP.txt`, `R2-Central.txt`: mau lenh CLI de cau hinh router tuong ung.

## Phan can thuc hien tiep trong Packet Tracer

Moi truong nay khong co Packet Tracer, vi vay chua co tep `.pkt`, ket qua kiem thu thuc te hoac anh minh chung. Hay dung topology trong Packet Tracer, nhap cau hinh, chay T1-T7, sau do thay cac muc `Chua thuc hien` trong report bang ket qua quan sat va tham chieu anh. Luu project thanh `MSSV_HoTenSinhVien_ND-Lab01.pkt` va xuat report hoan chinh thanh `MSSV_HoTenSinhVien_ND-Lab01.pdf` sau khi dien thong tin sinh vien.

Chi ghi kiem thu dat khi da chay trong Packet Tracer. Truoc khi nop, xem checklist o cuoi `NDLab01_Report.md`.# HD-Lab01
